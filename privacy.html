Who is responsible

Crumbsafe is run by Rickard Heron, a private individual in Sweden, who publishes it on Google Play under the developer name Nordic Mobile Co and is responsible (the data controller) for the processing described here.

Contact: nordicmobileco@gmail.com

In short

  • Your allergy profiles and settings stay on your device.
  • Barcode numbers are sent to Open Food Facts to look up products.
  • Label photos are read on your device and are not uploaded by Crumbsafe.
  • Usage analytics is off unless you allow it.
  • There are no ads and no advertising tracking.

Profiles and settings

What: profile names, selected allergens, language and theme, your terms and analytics choices, the birth year you give when asked about analytics, daily check counts, Plus access and saved copies of recently found products.

Why: to provide the app. Legal basis: performance of our agreement with you (the terms of service).

Where: only on your device. Allergy selections can be health information; Crumbsafe does not send them anywhere.

How long: until you change or delete them, use Settings → Delete all app data, or uninstall the app. Device backups are switched off for this app.

Barcode lookups

What: the barcode number you scan. Like any internet request, the lookup also reveals your IP address and basic technical details to the receiving service.

Camera: the camera is only used while the scanner is open. The camera image is processed on your device to find the barcode; it is not saved or sent anywhere. You can also type a barcode instead of using the camera.

Who receives it: Open Food Facts, an independent non-profit database based in France. Their own privacy policy applies to what they receive.

Why and legal basis: to show product information you ask for (performance of our agreement).

Only standard product barcodes (EAN/UPC) are looked up; QR codes and other codes are ignored.

If the product has a photo, the app loads it from Open Food Facts' image server, which also receives your IP address.

Found products (product information only) are saved on your device for up to 7 days, at most 50, so they can be shown if a later lookup fails. They are removed by Settings → Delete all app data.

Ingredient-label photos

When you check a label, the photo is taken with your camera and the text is read on your device by Google ML Kit. Crumbsafe does not upload the photo or the text. The app deletes its temporary photo files after use; this is not a guarantee of secure erasure.

Technical diagnostics from Google ML Kit

The on-device ML Kit libraries used for barcode scanning and label reading can send Google technical diagnostics and usage metrics, such as device model, operating-system version, app and library version and performance data. Google states that these do not include your photos or the recognised text. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in the scanner working reliably. Google's privacy policy applies.

Usage analytics (optional)

If you allow it, Google Analytics for Firebase collects app usage and device information: app opens, screens and engagement, app version, operating system, an app-instance identifier and approximate location derived from your network connection.

Crumbsafe also records: which introduction steps you complete; whether a barcode or label check found a product, read text, found nothing or failed (not what you scanned or the result for your allergens); when your welcome day starts and ends; when you reach the free daily limit; visits to the Plus screen, purchase starts and completed purchases (which product and its price, reported by Google Play); and coarse settings: language, theme, number of profiles (1, 2–3 or 4+) and whether you have Plus.

We do not add profile names, selected allergens, barcodes, product names, photos, label text or feedback to Analytics. Advertising-ID collection and ad-personalisation signals are switched off.

Who can allow it: analytics is only offered to users who are 16 or older, the highest age at which EU countries let people consent on their own. Before asking, the app asks for your birth year; the answer stays on your device and is used only for this.

Legal basis: your consent. You can withdraw it at any time under Settings → Usage analytics. Withdrawing stops future collection; it does not itself delete data already sent.

How long: detailed event data is deleted after 2 months. Data linked to the app-instance identifier is deleted 14 months after your last use of the app; each new use restarts that period. Google keeps aggregated, non-identifying statistics (such as total daily users) without a fixed limit.

Purchases

Payment for Crumbsafe Plus is handled by Google Play. The app only receives whether a purchase is pending, completed or restored, and stores your Plus access on the device. Legal basis: performance of our agreement. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment.

Feedback by email

The feedback option opens your own email app with a draft; nothing is sent until you send it. We receive what you write and your email address, and use it only to answer you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in handling your message. We delete feedback emails when they are no longer needed, and at the latest after 24 months.

Security

Crumbsafe has no servers of its own. Everything the app sends (barcode lookups, analytics if you allow it, and purchases through Google Play) travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Profiles and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read, and device backups are switched off for this app. No storage or transmission is completely secure, but the app only handles the data it needs to work.

Cookies and similar technologies

The app uses no cookies and no advertising identifiers. It stores necessary settings on your device. The analytics app-instance identifier is only used if you allow analytics, and is reset when you delete all app data.

Children

Crumbsafe is intended for people aged 13 and over. Parents and carers may create profiles for children; that information stays on the device. Usage analytics is never offered to users under 16. If you believe a child has allowed analytics anyway, contact us and we will have the data deleted.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. Recipients are limited to: Open Food Facts (barcode lookups), Google (Firebase Analytics if allowed, ML Kit diagnostics, Google Play purchases) and your email provider when you email us.

Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Google may process data in the United States. Google LLC participates in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and also uses the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Open Food Facts processes data in the EU.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Most data is only on your device, so you can view, change or delete it yourself in the app (Settings → Delete all app data removes everything). For anything else, contact nordicmobileco@gmail.com.

You can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY), www.imy.se, or the authority where you live.

Changes

We will update this policy when the app changes, and show the date above.
