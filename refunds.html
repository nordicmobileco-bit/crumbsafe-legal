Refunds through Google Play

Crumbsafe Plus is bought through Google Play. To request a refund, use your Google Play order history or Google Play's refund request page. Google Play decides those requests under its own refund policy; shortly after a purchase, Google Play often lets you request a refund directly.

If Plus does not work

If Plus does not work as described, contact us at nordicmobileco@gmail.com. We will try to fix the problem, and if we cannot, we will help you get a refund. This is in addition to your rights under consumer law.

Help

If you cannot find your purchase or need help, contact us. Never send payment-card details or passwords.
