Who we are

Crumbsafe is provided by Rickard Heron, a private individual in Sweden. Contact: nordicmobileco@gmail.com

Purpose

Crumbsafe helps you review available product information and text read from ingredient labels against the allergens you choose.

Important limitations

The app does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and cannot guarantee that food is safe. Product records and recognised text may be missing, incorrect, incomplete or outdated. A result showing no detected match does not mean that a product is allergen-free.

Your responsibility

Always read the physical packaging and follow your individual medical advice. Do not use the app as the only basis for deciding whether to eat a product. In an emergency, call 112 or your local emergency number.

Age

You must be at least 13 years old to use Crumbsafe. Parents and carers may use it to check products for children.

Profiles

You choose which allergens the app checks. Review the active profile and selected allergens before relying on a result.

Free use and Crumbsafe Plus

The free version includes a daily number of barcode and label checks. New users also get a welcome day: unlimited checks for 24 hours, starting from their first completed check. Crumbsafe Plus is a one-time purchase through Google Play that removes the daily limits for as long as the app is offered. It is not a subscription and has no recurring charges. The price, currency and any taxes are shown by Google Play before you confirm. Google Play's terms also apply to the purchase.

Right of withdrawal

Plus is digital content that is delivered immediately. When you confirm the purchase in Google Play, you agree that delivery starts at once and acknowledge that the 14-day right of withdrawal then ends. This does not affect your rights if Plus does not work as described; see the refund policy.
